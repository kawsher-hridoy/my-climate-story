# Decision 002: descriptive comparisons and explicit missing data

Status: accepted and implemented.

The supplied temperature analysis used unweighted months; POWER rainfall has a documented discontinuity; monthly data cannot support hot-day counts. Existing significance calculations are insufficient for authoritative local trend claims.

Use calendar-day annual temperature, complete-decade means, source-specific publication states and scoped review records. Do not force a warming result. GISTEMP stays global. Imagery is qualitative, checksum-reviewed and explicit about haze, sensors and masks.

Verified IMERG rainfall measurements are not included in the current release. Retain the chapter and importer with an explicit pending state, without fabricated values or a substituted POWER change claim. Daily-data and projection work require separately reviewed methods.
