# Sources, attribution and licenses

This is an independent educational application. NASA/USGS datasets and citations do not imply NASA endorsement or an official submission status.

## Scientific sources

- [NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/), NASA Langley Research Center: MERRA-2-derived monthly meteorology. See [API guidance](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/tutorials/service-data-request/api/).
- GISTEMP Team, 2026: [GISS Surface Temperature Analysis, version 4](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/), NASA GISS, accessed 2026-11-07.
- Lenssen, N., et al., 2024: *A GISTEMPv4 observational uncertainty ensemble*, Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres, doi:[10.1029/2023JD040179](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023JD040179).
- [NASA GPM IMERG Final monthly V07](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/ges-disc-gpm-3imergm-07), doi:10.5067/GPM/IMERG/3B-MONTH/07. Measurement acquisition is pending.
- [USGS/NASA Landsat Collection 2](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/spatiotemporal-asset-catalog-stac), public USGS metadata and imagery provenance.
- [Microsoft Planetary Computer Landsat hosting](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/landsat-c2-l2), used to retrieve matching source COG windows. Microsoft is the host, not the satellite producer.
- [NASA GIBS](https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/access-basics/), regional MODIS imagery with visible map attribution.

Record individual scene IDs/dates and the data release when citing a comparison. Processed RGB images are derivatives, not newly observed NASA measurements.

## Educational content and visual assets

Educational copy is original bilingual paraphrase grounded in [NASA Earth resources](https://science.nasa.gov/kids/earth/) and [UNICEF Bangladesh heat-safety guidance](https://www.unicef.org/bangladesh/en/press-releases/children-are-high-risk-amid-countrywide-heatwave-bangladesh).

The hero landscape and app icon are original code-native illustrations; they are not satellite evidence. Icons use Lucide (ISC license). DM Sans and Noto Sans Bengali are distributed with their font-source packages and SIL Open Font License terms. Preserve upstream notices when redistributing their assets.

No AI-generated raster is used as scientific imagery. The original PDF/ZIP are retained as project inputs; this repository does not assert ownership of third-party source material.
