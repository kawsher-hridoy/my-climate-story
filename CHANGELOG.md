# Changelog

## 0.1.0

### Learning experience

- Bangla/English interface and twelve curated Bangladesh stories.
- Twenty-four real, aligned, QA-checked, checksum-reviewed Landsat images.
- Regional 1981–2025 temperature records, reviewed descriptive comparisons, tables, CSV, and separate GISTEMP global context.
- Worldwide place/coordinate exploration with provisional temperature retrieval.
- Bilingual quizzes, source-backed image questions, local progress, and relevant action cards.
- Offline story packs, persistent owned media, migration/repair, and classroom printing.

### Engineering

- FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Alembic, and a durable Procrastinate worker.
- Transactional job dispatch, bounded recovery, leases, and stale-worker fencing.
- Complete verified result bundles and immutable format 2 scientific releases.
- Place-specific series provenance and source- and checksum-bound publication reviews.
- Proxy-aware client limits, private API/database ports, and safely quoted database credentials.
- Docker Compose/Caddy deployment, scientific pipelines, and reproducible data/schema references.

### Limitations

Verified IMERG rainfall measurements remain pending. Arbitrary global points do not automatically receive approved imagery or change comparisons. Accounts, uploads, daily extreme indices, forecasts, and messaging are outside this release.
