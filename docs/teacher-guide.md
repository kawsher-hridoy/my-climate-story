# Classroom guide · শ্রেণিকক্ষের নির্দেশিকা

Audience: grades 6–10. Duration: 35 minutes. Materials: one connected device or a previously saved story, paper and pencils. No accounts or special equipment are needed.

## Learning goals

Students distinguish weather from climate, read source dates/units, describe visible observations, recognize regional estimates and identify questions that require more evidence. They select a safe, realistic action without claiming an unmeasured climate outcome.

## Lesson

| Time | Activity | Discussion |
|---|---|---|
| 5 minutes | Choose a familiar place | What do you expect to see? Which landmarks matter to your community? |
| 8 minutes | Compare images | What changed visibly? Which color differences might come from season, haze or sensors? |
| 8 minutes | Read temperature and sources | What unit, area and years are represented? What is different about global anomalies? |
| 7 minutes | Play the quiz | Read every explanation, including incorrect answers. What would need daily data? |
| 7 minutes | Write a question and choose an action | What can we safely try with our school or families? |

Discuss the pending rainfall chapter. It is an example of responsible data use: a missing or problematic record is not replaced with invented certainty.

## Worksheet

Have each student record:

1. My selected place and the two acquisition dates.
2. Three observations I can point to in an image or chart.
3. The source, unit, period and represented area.
4. One explanation that needs additional evidence.
5. One safe action to try together.

The app's Teachers page provides a printable worksheet. Print a story if a shared screen is not available. A saved pack supports both languages without a connection.

## Facilitation

Avoid treating every local record as a definite warming result. A descriptive decade difference is not a significance test or hot-day count. Do not label a city-center crop as mangrove/coastline evidence if those features are absent.

Encourage students to explain why they chose an answer. Quiz results are local, informal learning feedback rather than an assessment database. Field activities require adult planning; students should not enter floodwater or approach unstable riverbanks.

## বাংলায় সংক্ষিপ্ত নির্দেশনা

একটি চেনা জায়গা বেছে নাও। শিক্ষার্থীদের আগে পর্যবেক্ষণ বলতে দাও, তারপর ব্যাখ্যার জন্য কী তথ্য দরকার জিজ্ঞাসা করো। ছবি ও চার্টের তারিখ, উৎস, একক আর যে এলাকা বোঝায় তা পড়াও। অনুপস্থিত বৃষ্টির তথ্যকে তথ্যের সীমা বোঝানোর উদাহরণ হিসেবে ব্যবহার করো। কুইজের প্রতিটি উত্তরের ব্যাখ্যা আলোচনা করো। শেষে পরিবার বা ক্লাসের সঙ্গে করার একটি নিরাপদ কাজ লিখতে দাও।
